Goals学习目标Learning goals

  • 把 v 拆成两半：沿轴的部分 v∥ = (k · v) k 转动时完全不动；垂直于轴的部分 v⊥ = v − v∥ 在一个垂直于轴的平面里画圆，圆心在 v∥ 的尖端，半径是 |v⊥|。（k 是单位向量。）
  • 在那个平面里做一次 2D 旋转：平面里有两条现成的互相垂直的轴：v⊥ 和 k × v（它就是 v⊥ 绕 k 转了 90°）。所以 v′ = v∥ + cosθ · v⊥ + sinθ · (k × v)。θ 的正方向用右手定则：拇指指向 k，四指弯的方向为正。
  • 把它写成矩阵，并会反过来读：R = cosθ · I + sinθ · K + (1 − cosθ) · k kᵀ，其中 K 是「叉积矩阵」（K v = k × v），k kᵀ 是「投影矩阵」。R 的三列是 x、y、z 轴转过去的样子；R 是正交矩阵（Rᵀ = R⁻¹，det = +1）；对角线之和 = 1 + 2cosθ，可以读出转角。
  • Split v in two: the part along the axis, v∥ = (k · v) k, does not move at all; the part perpendicular to the axis, v⊥ = v − v∥, traces a circle in a plane perpendicular to the axis, centered at the tip of v∥ with radius |v⊥|. (k is a unit vector.)
  • Do a 2D rotation in that plane: the plane already has two perpendicular axes: v⊥ and k × v (which is v⊥ turned 90° about k). So v′ = v∥ + cosθ · v⊥ + sinθ · (k × v). The positive direction of θ follows the right-hand rule: thumb along k, the fingers curl in the positive direction.
  • Write it as a matrix and read it back: R = cosθ · I + sinθ · K + (1 − cosθ) · k kᵀ, where K is the "cross-product matrix" (K v = k × v) and k kᵀ is the "projection matrix". R's three columns are where the x, y, z axes go; R is orthogonal (Rᵀ = R⁻¹, det = +1); the sum of its diagonal is 1 + 2cosθ, which tells you the angle.

Intuition直觉Intuition

想象烤串：签子是轴 k，串在上面的肉块绕着签子转。签子上的点不动；离签子越远的点，画的圆越大。每个点都在一个和签子垂直的圆盘里转圈，圆心就在签子上，正对着这个点。

Picture a skewer: the stick is the axis k and the food on it turns around the stick. Points on the stick do not move; the farther a point is from the stick, the bigger its circle. Every point turns in a disk perpendicular to the stick, and the disk's center is on the stick, right across from the point.

所以绕任意轴旋转只需要三步：(1) 找到圆心，也就是 v 在轴上的投影（1.2 课的点积）；(2) 在圆盘里找两条互相垂直的方向：一条是从圆心指向 v 的 v⊥，另一条是把它转 90° 的 k × v（1.3 课的叉积）；(3) 在这两条方向组成的平面里做 1.5 课学过的 2D 旋转。Rodrigues 公式就是这三步写在一行里。

So rotating about any axis takes three steps: (1) find the circle's center, the projection of v onto the axis (the dot product from lesson 1.2); (2) find two perpendicular directions in the disk: v⊥ from the center to v, and k × v, which is v⊥ turned 90° (the cross product from lesson 1.3); (3) do the 2D rotation from lesson 1.5 in the plane those two directions span. Rodrigues' formula is those three steps on one line.

1.6 课的 rotationZ 是这个公式的特例：k = (0, 0, 1) 时，v∥ 就是 z 分量，v⊥ 是 (x, y, 0)，k × v = (−y, x, 0)，代进去正好是 rotationZ。

Lesson 1.6's rotationZ is a special case: with k = (0, 0, 1), v∥ is the z part, v⊥ is (x, y, 0) and k × v = (−y, x, 0); plug them in and you get exactly rotationZ.

Why为什么公式长这样？Why does the formula look like this?

1. 几何：顺着轴往下看，就是 1.5 课的 2D 旋转

1. Geometry: look down the axis and it is the 2D rotation from lesson 1.5

站在 k 的尖端，朝原点往下看。v∥ 缩成了一个点（圆心），v⊥ 是一条半径，k × v 是把它逆时针转 90° 的另一条半径。这两条半径一样长、互相垂直，正好当作这个平面里的「x 轴」和「y 轴」。在这个坐标系里，转 θ 后的点就是 (cosθ, sinθ)，也就是 cosθ · v⊥ + sinθ · (k × v)。拖动 θ 看看。

Stand at the tip of k and look down toward the origin. v∥ shrinks to a point (the center), v⊥ is one radius and k × v is that radius turned 90° counter-clockwise. The two radii have the same length and are perpendicular, so they serve as this plane's "x axis" and "y axis". In that frame the point after turning by θ is (cosθ, sinθ), i.e. cosθ · v⊥ + sinθ · (k × v). Drag θ to see it.

为什么 k × v 正好是 v⊥ 转了 90°？叉积 k × v 垂直于 k（所以在这个平面里）、垂直于 v（所以垂直于 v⊥），长度是 |k| |v| sin(夹角) = |v⊥|（1.3 课：叉积的长度是平行四边形面积，底 |k| = 1，高正是 |v⊥|）。方向由右手定则定：从 k 尖端看，是逆时针转 90°。

Why is k × v exactly v⊥ turned 90°? The cross product k × v is perpendicular to k (so it lies in the plane) and to v (so it is perpendicular to v⊥), and its length is |k| |v| sin(angle) = |v⊥| (lesson 1.3: the length of a cross product is a parallelogram's area; the base |k| is 1 and the height is exactly |v⊥|). Its direction comes from the right-hand rule: seen from the tip of k, it is a 90° counter-clockwise turn.

2. 代数：从向量形式到矩阵形式

2. Algebra: from the vector form to the matrix form

公式里每一项都是 v 的线性函数，所以整个公式能写成一个矩阵乘 v。只要把每一项都写成「某个矩阵乘 v」：

Every term in the formula is a linear function of v, so the whole formula can be written as one matrix times v. Just write each term as "some matrix times v":

cosθ · v            = (cosθ · I) v
sinθ · (k × v)      = (sinθ · K) v                 K 把叉积写成矩阵 / K writes the cross product as a matrix
(k · v) k = k (kᵀ v) = (k kᵀ) v                    换个括号 / regroup the brackets
⇒ v′ = [ cosθ · I + sinθ · K + (1 − cosθ) · k kᵀ ] v = R v

第三行的关键是把 (k · v) k 看成 k · (kᵀ v)，再把括号换到前面：(k kᵀ) v。k kᵀ 是一个 3 × 1 的列乘 1 × 3 的行，结果是 3 × 3 的矩阵（外积）。另一种常见写法用 K² = k kᵀ − I（推导见补课 C），得到 R = I + sinθ · K + (1 − cosθ) · K²。

The key in line three is to see (k · v) k as k · (kᵀ v) and move the brackets: (k kᵀ) v. k kᵀ is a 3 × 1 column times a 1 × 3 row, which gives a 3 × 3 matrix (the outer product). Another common form uses K² = k kᵀ − I (derived in primer C) and gives R = I + sinθ · K + (1 − cosθ) · K².

3. 物理：无数次「微小转动」加起来

3. Physics: the sum of countless tiny turns

刚体以角速度 ω 绕轴 k 转动时，上面一点的速度是 ω k × v（物理课的 v = ω × r）。如果把转动 θ 分成 n 小步，每一步都沿着当前速度方向直走一小段：v ← v + (θ / n) · k × v。步数少时，路径是一个往外张开的多边形（每一步都走在切线上，会稍微远离圆心）；步数越多，越贴近真正的圆，终点越接近 Rodrigues 公式的结果。Rodrigues 公式就是 n → ∞ 时这个过程的精确答案。

When a rigid body spins about the axis k with angular speed ω, a point on it has velocity ω k × v (the physics formula v = ω × r). Split the turn θ into n small steps and walk straight along the current velocity each time: v ← v + (θ / n) · k × v. With few steps the path is a polygon that spirals outward (every step runs along a tangent and drifts slightly away from the center); with more steps it hugs the true circle and the end point approaches Rodrigues' result. Rodrigues' formula is the exact answer as n → ∞.

这也解释了为什么物理引擎和游戏里一般不用「v += ω × v · Δt」来更新朝向：误差会让物体越转越大（长度不守恒）。正确的做法是每一帧用 Rodrigues 公式（或下一课的四元数）算出这一小段旋转，再乘上去。

This is also why physics engines and games do not usually update orientation with "v += ω × v · Δt": the error makes the object grow as it spins (length is not preserved). The right way is to compute each frame's small rotation with Rodrigues' formula (or with next lesson's quaternions) and multiply it in.

A. 把叉积写成矩阵 K

A. The cross product as a matrix K

固定 k，把 v 变成 k × v 是一个线性变换（v 加倍，结果也加倍；两个 v 相加，结果也相加）。线性变换都能写成矩阵，而 1.5 课说过：矩阵的第 j 列就是第 j 个基向量变过去的样子。所以 K 的三列就是 k × x̂、k × ŷ、k × ẑ。下面用 k = (1, 2, 3) 一列一列算。

With k fixed, turning v into k × v is a linear map (double v and the result doubles; add two v's and the results add). Every linear map is a matrix, and lesson 1.5 said that column j of the matrix is where the j-th basis vector goes. So K's three columns are k × x̂, k × ŷ and k × ẑ. Below we compute them column by column for k = (1, 2, 3).

B. 外积 k kᵀ：一个做投影的矩阵

B. The outer product k kᵀ: a matrix that projects

点积 kᵀ v 是「行 × 列」，得到一个数（内积）。反过来「列 × 行」k kᵀ 得到一个 3 × 3 矩阵（外积），第 i 行第 j 列是 kᵢ · kⱼ。它的作用是投影：(k kᵀ) v = k (kᵀ v) = (k · v) k = v∥。下面用单位向量 k = (2, 1, 2) / 3 来算。

The dot product kᵀ v is "row × column" and gives a number (the inner product). The other way round, "column × row" k kᵀ gives a 3 × 3 matrix (the outer product) whose row i, column j entry is kᵢ · kⱼ. What it does is project: (k kᵀ) v = k (kᵀ v) = (k · v) k = v∥. Below we use the unit vector k = (2, 1, 2) / 3.

C. 叉两次：k × (k × v) = −v⊥

C. Crossing twice: k × (k × v) = −v⊥

几何：k × 把 v⊥ 转 90°，同时把 v∥ 消掉（平行向量的叉积是 0）。再叉一次，又转 90°，一共转了 180°，所以得到 −v⊥。

Geometry: k × turns v⊥ by 90° and kills v∥ (the cross product of parallel vectors is 0). Cross again and it turns another 90°, 180° in total, so the result is −v⊥.

代数：用三重积展开公式 a × (b × c) = b (a · c) − c (a · b)（记法：BAC − CAB）。取 a = b = k、c = v：k × (k × v) = k (k · v) − v (k · k) = v∥ − v = −v⊥（因为 k · k = 1）。写成矩阵就是 K² v = (k kᵀ − I) v，所以 K² = k kᵀ − I。把它代进 R = cosθ · I + sinθ · K + (1 − cosθ) · k kᵀ，就得到另一种写法 R = I + sinθ · K + (1 − cosθ) · K²。

Algebra: use the triple product expansion a × (b × c) = b (a · c) − c (a · b) (mnemonic: BAC − CAB). With a = b = k and c = v: k × (k × v) = k (k · v) − v (k · k) = v∥ − v = −v⊥ (because k · k = 1). As matrices that is K² v = (k kᵀ − I) v, so K² = k kᵀ − I. Substitute it into R = cosθ · I + sinθ · K + (1 − cosθ) · k kᵀ and you get the other form R = I + sinθ · K + (1 − cosθ) · K².

手算验证：k = (0, 0, 1)，v = (3, 4, 5)。k × v = (−4, 3, 0)，k × (−4, 3, 0) = (−3, −4, 0)。而 v⊥ = (3, 4, 0)，确实是 −v⊥。

Check by hand: k = (0, 0, 1), v = (3, 4, 5). k × v = (−4, 3, 0) and k × (−4, 3, 0) = (−3, −4, 0). Since v⊥ = (3, 4, 0), that is indeed −v⊥.

D. 组装 R，并检查它

D. Assemble R and check it

用 B 里的 k = (2, 1, 2) / 3，转 θ = 90°。cos 90° = 0、sin 90° = 1，所以 R = K + k kᵀ。为了方便，所有数都写成「几分之九」。

Use B's k = (2, 1, 2) / 3 and turn θ = 90°. cos 90° = 0 and sin 90° = 1, so R = K + k kᵀ. For convenience every number is written in ninths.

E. 正交矩阵：怎么判断「这是一个旋转」

E. Orthogonal matrices: how to tell "this is a rotation"

怎么算：检查 Rᵀ R = I，也就是三列都是单位长度、两两垂直（Rᵀ R 的第 i 行第 j 列正好是第 i 列和第 j 列的点积）。再检查 det R = +1。

How to check: verify Rᵀ R = I, i.e. the three columns have unit length and are pairwise perpendicular (row i, column j of Rᵀ R is exactly the dot product of column i and column j). Then check det R = +1.

为什么：旋转不改变长度和夹角，所以 x̂、ŷ、ẑ 转过去以后仍然是三条互相垂直的单位向量，也就是 R 的三列。det = +1 说明没有翻面（1.5 课：det 的正负是朝向）；三列正交但 det = −1 的是镜像，比如 diag(−1, 1, 1)。

Why: a rotation preserves lengths and angles, so after turning, x̂, ŷ, ẑ are still three perpendicular unit vectors, and those are R's columns. det = +1 means nothing was flipped (lesson 1.5: the sign of det is orientation); orthonormal columns with det = −1 is a mirror, such as diag(−1, 1, 1).

本质：Rᵀ = R⁻¹，求逆只要转置，几乎不花计算。用 Rodrigues 公式也能看出来：K 是反对称的（Kᵀ = −K），k kᵀ 是对称的，所以 Rᵀ = cosθ · I − sinθ · K + (1 − cosθ) · k kᵀ = R(k, −θ)：转置就是倒着转。这也是 1.7 课「旋转的逆转置就是它自己」的原因：(R⁻¹)ᵀ = (Rᵀ)ᵀ = R。

What it really is: Rᵀ = R⁻¹, so inverting costs almost nothing, just a transpose. Rodrigues' formula shows it too: K is antisymmetric (Kᵀ = −K) and k kᵀ is symmetric, so Rᵀ = cosθ · I − sinθ · K + (1 − cosθ) · k kᵀ = R(k, −θ): the transpose turns backwards. That is also why, in lesson 1.7, a rotation's inverse transpose is itself: (R⁻¹)ᵀ = (Rᵀ)ᵀ = R.

读出转角：对角线之和叫迹（trace）。I 的迹是 3，K 的迹是 0，k kᵀ 的迹是 kx² + ky² + kz² = 1，所以 tr R = 3cosθ + 0 + (1 − cosθ) = 1 + 2cosθ。D 里的例子迹是 (4 + 1 + 4) / 9 = 1，cosθ = 0，θ = 90°，对得上。

Reading the angle: the sum of the diagonal is the trace. I has trace 3, K has trace 0 and k kᵀ has trace kx² + ky² + kz² = 1, so tr R = 3cosθ + 0 + (1 − cosθ) = 1 + 2cosθ. The example in D has trace (4 + 1 + 4) / 9 = 1, so cosθ = 0 and θ = 90°, as expected.

读出转轴：轴上的向量不动：R k = k。另外 R − Rᵀ = 2sinθ · K（对称部分抵消了），从 K 的三个元素就能读出 k（θ = 180° 时 sinθ = 0，这个办法失效，要改用 R + I 的列）。

Reading the axis: vectors on the axis do not move: R k = k. Also R − Rᵀ = 2sinθ · K (the symmetric parts cancel), so k can be read off K's three entries (at θ = 180°, sinθ = 0 and this fails; use the columns of R + I instead).

FAQ你可能会问You might ask

1. 公式里为什么有个 (1 − cosθ)？它是什么意思？1. Why is there a (1 − cosθ) in the formula? What does it mean?

它来自「v∥ 不动」。写成 v′ = cosθ · v + sinθ · (k × v) + (1 − cosθ) v∥：第一项把整个 v（包括 v∥）都乘了 cosθ，可 v∥ 本来不该缩短，所以要把少掉的 (1 − cosθ) v∥ 补回来。θ = 0 时补 0；θ = 180° 时 cosθ = −1，第一项把 v∥ 翻成了 −v∥，要补 2v∥ 才能翻回来。

It comes from "v∥ stays put". Write v′ = cosθ · v + sinθ · (k × v) + (1 − cosθ) v∥: the first term multiplies all of v, including v∥, by cosθ, but v∥ should not shrink, so the missing (1 − cosθ) v∥ is added back. At θ = 0 nothing is added; at θ = 180°, cosθ = −1, the first term flips v∥ to −v∥, and 2v∥ must be added to flip it back.

2. 它和「先把轴转到 z，绕 z 转，再转回来」是什么关系？2. How does it relate to "turn the axis onto z, rotate about z, turn back"?

结果完全一样。那是 1.6 课三明治的旋转版：R = A · Rz(θ) · A⁻¹，其中 A 把 z 轴转到 k，而 A⁻¹ = Aᵀ（补课 E）。只是你得先找出 A（需要两个和 k 垂直的方向），还要乘三次矩阵。Rodrigues 公式等于把这个三明治提前乘好了，直接用 k 和 θ 就能写出 R。UC Davis 第 04 讲就是先讲三明治做法，再化简出这个公式。

The result is exactly the same. It is the rotation version of lesson 1.6's sandwich: R = A · Rz(θ) · A⁻¹, where A turns the z axis onto k and A⁻¹ = Aᵀ (primer E). But you first have to find A (it needs two directions perpendicular to k) and then multiply three matrices. Rodrigues' formula is that sandwich multiplied out in advance, so R can be written straight from k and θ. UC Davis lecture 04 starts with the sandwich and simplifies it into this formula.

3. 为什么 k 一定要是单位向量？忘了归一化会怎样？3. Why must k be a unit vector? What happens if I forget to normalize?

推导里用了两次 |k| = 1：v∥ = (k · v) k 只有在 |k| = 1 时才是投影（否则多乘了 |k|²）；k × v 的长度等于 |v⊥| 也要求 |k| = 1。k = (0, 0, 2) 时，算出来的「旋转」会把向量拉长，R 不再是正交矩阵。所以代码第一步总是 k = k.normalize()。

The derivation used |k| = 1 twice: v∥ = (k · v) k is a projection only when |k| = 1 (otherwise there is an extra factor |k|²), and the length of k × v equals |v⊥| only when |k| = 1. With k = (0, 0, 2) the "rotation" stretches vectors and R is no longer orthogonal. So the first line of the code is always k = k.normalize().

4. 绕 −k 转 θ 和绕 k 转 −θ 一样吗？θ 的正方向到底怎么定？4. Is turning by θ about −k the same as turning by −θ about k? Which way is positive?

一样。公式里 k 换成 −k：K 变号，k kᵀ 不变，效果和 sinθ 变号相同。所以「轴 + 角」这种表示有两个名字：(k, θ) 和 (−k, −θ) 是同一个旋转。正方向用右手定则：右手拇指指向 k，四指弯曲的方向就是正 θ；等价地说，从 k 的尖端往原点看，正 θ 是逆时针。这和 1.6 课 rotationZ 的约定一致（从 +z 往下看，正角度把 x 轴转向 y 轴）。

Yes. Replace k with −k in the formula: K changes sign while k kᵀ does not, which has the same effect as flipping the sign of sinθ. So the axis-angle representation has two names for the same rotation: (k, θ) and (−k, −θ). The positive direction is the right-hand rule: point your right thumb along k, and the way your fingers curl is positive θ; equivalently, looking from the tip of k toward the origin, positive θ is counter-clockwise. This matches lesson 1.6's rotationZ (seen from +z, a positive angle turns the x axis toward the y axis).

5. 轴不经过原点怎么办？比如门绕门轴转。5. What if the axis does not pass through the origin, like a door on its hinge?

用 1.6 课的三明治：M = T(p) · R(k, θ) · T(−p)，p 是轴上任意一点。先把 p 移到原点，绕过原点的轴转，再移回去。写的顺序和执行顺序相反，最先做的 T(−p) 写在最右边。这就是练习的第三个方法。

Use lesson 1.6's sandwich: M = T(p) · R(k, θ) · T(−p), where p is any point on the axis. Move p to the origin, rotate about the axis through the origin, move back. Written order is the reverse of execution order: T(−p), which happens first, goes on the far right. This is the exercise's third method.

欧拉公式的 3D 版Euler's formula, in 3D

你可能见过欧拉公式 e^(iθ) = cosθ + i · sinθ，它在复平面上表示「转 θ」，关键是 i² = −1。K 有一个类似的性质：K³ = −K（补课 C 里 K² = k kᵀ − I，再乘 K，因为 k kᵀ K = 0，得到 K³ = −K），也就是在垂直于轴的平面里，K 的作用和 i 完全一样：转 90°，转两次变成负号。

You may have seen Euler's formula e^(iθ) = cosθ + i · sinθ, which means "turn by θ" in the complex plane; the key is i² = −1. K has a similar property: K³ = −K (from primer C, K² = k kᵀ − I; multiply by K, and since k kᵀ K = 0 you get K³ = −K). In the plane perpendicular to the axis, K acts exactly like i: a 90° turn, and two of them give a minus sign.

所以把指数函数的级数 e^(θK) = I + θK + (θK)²/2! + (θK)³/3! + … 展开，用 K³ = −K 把高次项全部化简，奇数次项凑成 sinθ · K，偶数次项凑成 (1 − cosθ) · K²，正好得到 R = I + sinθ · K + (1 − cosθ) · K²。Rodrigues 公式就是矩阵版的 e^(iθ)。上面「物理」那张图里步数 n → ∞ 的极限，也正是这个指数：(I + θK / n)ⁿ → e^(θK)。

So expand the exponential series e^(θK) = I + θK + (θK)²/2! + (θK)³/3! + …, reduce every high power with K³ = −K, and the odd powers collect into sinθ · K while the even powers collect into (1 − cosθ) · K², giving exactly R = I + sinθ · K + (1 − cosθ) · K². Rodrigues' formula is a matrix version of e^(iθ). The n → ∞ limit in the physics figure above is this same exponential: (I + θK / n)ⁿ → e^(θK).

还有一段历史：Olinde Rodrigues 在 1840 年发表的论文里，研究了两个旋转怎么合成，用的是四个参数，这和 1843 年 Hamilton 发明的四元数乘法本质相同，早了三年，但长期没人注意。Simon Altmann 1989 年在 Mathematics Magazine 上写过一篇文章专门讲这段往事，标题就叫 Hamilton, Rodrigues, and the Quaternion Scandal。下一课就讲四元数。

There is history too: in a paper published in 1840, Olinde Rodrigues worked out how two rotations combine, using four parameters. This is essentially the quaternion multiplication that Hamilton discovered in 1843, three years earlier, but it went unnoticed for a long time. Simon Altmann told the story in a 1989 Mathematics Magazine article titled Hamilton, Rodrigues, and the Quaternion Scandal. Quaternions are the next lesson.

      参考答案Reference answer 
      rotateAxis(k, theta) {                               // 写在 Vec3 里 / inside Vec3
  const u = k.normalize(), c = Math.cos(theta), s = Math.sin(theta);
  return this.scale(c).add(u.cross(this).scale(s)).add(u.scale((1 - c) * u.dot(this)));
}
static rotationAxis(k, theta) {
  const u = k.normalize(), x = u.x, y = u.y, z = u.z;
  const c = Math.cos(theta), s = Math.sin(theta), t = 1 - c;
  return new Mat4([
    c + t*x*x,    t*x*y - s*z,  t*x*z + s*y,  0,
    t*x*y + s*z,  c + t*y*y,    t*y*z - s*x,  0,
    t*x*z - s*y,  t*y*z + s*x,  c + t*z*z,    0,
    0,            0,            0,            1]);
}
static rotationAboutLine(p, k, theta) {                // 最右边的 T(−p) 最先做 / the rightmost T(−p) happens first
  return Mat4.translation(p.x, p.y, p.z).mul(Mat4.rotationAxis(k, theta)).mul(Mat4.translation(-p.x, -p.y, -p.z));
}

      Pitfalls常见误区Common mistakes

      • 忘了把 k 归一化。结果会被拉长，R 不再正交。three.js 的 Matrix4.makeRotationAxis(axis, angle) 文档也写明 axis 必须是单位向量。
      • 把矩阵写成了转置。行优先数组里，第 1 行第 2 列是 m[1]，第 2 行第 1 列是 m[4]。把 −s·z 和 +s·z 放反，得到的是 Rᵀ = R(k, −θ)：转的方向反了，而且看起来「也是个旋转」，很难发现。测试一定要检查一个已知方向，比如绕 z 轴转 90° 时 x 轴要到 y 轴。
      • 正方向搞反。正 θ 是从 k 的尖端往回看的逆时针。k 和 −k 是两个不同的轴方向，转的方向正好相反。
      • 角度单位。Math.cos 要弧度。UI 里用度，进公式前乘 π / 180（1.2 课犯过一次）。
      • 轴不过原点时直接用 R。要用 T(p) · R · T(−p)，而且 T(−p) 在最右边。
      • Forgetting to normalize k. The result gets stretched and R is no longer orthogonal. three.js's docs for Matrix4.makeRotationAxis(axis, angle) also say the axis must be a unit vector.
      • Writing the transpose by mistake. In a row-major array, row 1 column 2 is m[1] and row 2 column 1 is m[4]. Swap −s·z and +s·z and you get Rᵀ = R(k, −θ): it turns the wrong way and still "looks like a rotation", which makes it hard to spot. Always test one known direction, e.g. a 90° turn about z must send the x axis to the y axis.
      • Getting the positive direction backwards. Positive θ is counter-clockwise when looking back from the tip of k. k and −k are different axis directions and turn opposite ways.
      • Angle units. Math.cos wants radians. If the UI uses degrees, multiply by π / 180 before the formula (this happened once in lesson 1.2).
      • Using R directly when the axis misses the origin. Use T(p) · R · T(−p), with T(−p) on the far right.

      2. 绕 k = (1, 1, 1) / √3 转 120°，x 轴会被送到哪里？为什么？2. Turning 120° about k = (1, 1, 1) / √3, where does the x axis go, and why?

      到 y 轴（y 到 z，z 到 x）。直观上：从 (1, 1, 1) 方向往原点看，x、y、z 三个轴的尖端是一个正三角形，彼此相隔 120°，逆时针转 120° 正好轮换一格。代公式：k · x̂ = 1/√3，k × x̂ = (0, 1, −1)/√3，cos 120° = −1/2，sin 120° = √3/2，v′ = −½(1, 0, 0) + ½(0, 1, −1) + (3/2)(1/3)(1, 1, 1) = (0, 1, 0)。

      To the y axis (y goes to z, z to x). Intuitively: looking from (1, 1, 1) toward the origin, the tips of the x, y, z axes form an equilateral triangle, 120° apart, so a 120° counter-clockwise turn shifts each one place. By the formula: k · x̂ = 1/√3, k × x̂ = (0, 1, −1)/√3, cos 120° = −1/2, sin 120° = √3/2, and v′ = −½(1, 0, 0) + ½(0, 1, −1) + (3/2)(1/3)(1, 1, 1) = (0, 1, 0).

      迹 = 0 = 1 + 2cosθ，cosθ = −1/2，θ = 120°（比如第 2 题那个轮换矩阵）。它的转置就是它的逆，也就是绕同一个轴倒着转 120°。

      Trace = 0 = 1 + 2cosθ, so cosθ = −1/2 and θ = 120° (for example the cycling matrix of question 2). Its transpose is its inverse: a 120° turn backwards about the same axis.
