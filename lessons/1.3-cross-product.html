Goals学习目标Learning goals

  • 方向和顺序：知道 a × b 是一个同时垂直于 a 和 b 的向量，会用右手定则判断它朝哪边，知道 b × a = −(a × b)。
  • 大小：知道 |a × b| = |a| |b| sin θ，等于 a、b 张成的平行四边形面积；平行时为 0。
  • 用途：会用 (B − A) × (C − A) 求三角形的法线和面积，知道顶点顺序（绕序）决定法线朝外还是朝里。
  • Direction and order: know that a × b is a vector perpendicular to both a and b, find its direction with the right-hand rule, and know that b × a = −(a × b).
  • Size: know that |a × b| = |a| |b| sin θ, the area of the parallelogram spanned by a and b, and 0 when they are parallel.
  • Use: get a triangle's normal and area from (B − A) × (C − A), and know that the vertex order (winding) decides whether the normal points out or in.

Intuition直觉Intuition

点积回答"两支箭头有多同向"，给出一个数。叉积回答另一个问题：两支箭头张开了多大一块面，这块面朝哪边？它给出一个向量：方向垂直于那块面，长度等于面积。

The dot product answers "how much do two arrows agree" with a number. The cross product answers a different question: how big a patch do two arrows span, and which way does that patch face? It gives a vector whose direction is perpendicular to the patch and whose length is its area.

右手定则：伸出右手，四指指向 a，再朝 b 的方向弯过去，大拇指指的就是 a × b。反过来从 b 弯向 a，拇指就朝相反方向，所以 b × a = −(a × b)。

Right-hand rule: point the fingers of your right hand along a, curl them toward b, and your thumb points along a × b. Curl from b to a instead and your thumb flips, so b × a = −(a × b).

图形学里最常见的用途是三角形的法线：模型由成千上万个三角形组成，光照（1.2 的 n · l）需要知道每个面朝哪边，这个方向就是用两条边的叉积算出来的。

Its most common use in graphics is the triangle normal: models are made of thousands of triangles, lighting (the n · l of lesson 1.2) needs to know which way each one faces, and that direction comes from the cross product of two edges.

Math数学Math

a × b = (a_y b_z − a_z b_y,
         a_z bₓ − aₓ b_z,
         aₓ b_y − a_y bₓ)

|a × b| = |a| |b| sin θ            ← 平行四边形面积 / parallelogram area
a × b ⟂ a,  a × b ⟂ b              ← 垂直于两者 / perpendicular to both
b × a = −(a × b)                   ← 交换顺序，方向反过来 / swap the order, flip the direction
a × a = 0                          ← 平行时为零 / zero when parallel

记分量的办法：x 分量只用 y、z（"yz − zy"），y 分量用 z、x（"zx − xz"），z 分量用 x、y（"xy − yx"），下标按 x → y → z → x 循环。2D 里只有 z 分量 aₓ b_y − a_y bₓ：它的正负告诉你 b 在 a 的左边（正，逆时针）还是右边（负，顺时针）。

How to remember the components: the x component uses only y and z ("yz − zy"), y uses z and x ("zx − xz"), z uses x and y ("xy − yx"); the indices cycle x → y → z → x. In 2D only the z component aₓ b_y − a_y bₓ remains, and its sign tells you whether b is to the left of a (positive, counter-clockwise) or to the right (negative, clockwise).

三角形：顶点 A、B、C 的法线方向是 n = (B − A) × (C − A)，面积是 |n| / 2（三角形是平行四边形的一半）。单位法线是 n / |n|。顶点按逆时针排列时（从外面看），法线朝外；three.js 和 WebGL 默认把逆时针的那一面当作正面。

Triangles: for vertices A, B, C the normal direction is n = (B − A) × (C − A) and the area is |n| / 2 (a triangle is half a parallelogram). The unit normal is n / |n|. When the vertices run counter-clockwise as seen from outside, the normal points out; three.js and WebGL treat the counter-clockwise side as the front face by default.

Why为什么这个公式垂直于 a 和 b，长度又是面积？Why is this formula perpendicular, with the area as its length?

1. 垂直：直接用点积验证

1. Perpendicular: check with the dot product

把 a 和 a × b 点乘：aₓ(a_y b_z − a_z b_y) + a_y(a_z bₓ − aₓ b_z) + a_z(aₓ b_y − a_y bₓ)。展开后六项两两抵消，结果是 0。由 1.2 课，点积为 0 就是垂直。对 b 同理。公式里那些"交叉"的下标，正是为了让这些项能互相抵消。

Dot a with a × b: aₓ(a_y b_z − a_z b_y) + a_y(a_z bₓ − aₓ b_z) + a_z(aₓ b_y − a_y bₓ). Expand it and the six terms cancel in pairs, leaving 0. By lesson 1.2, a zero dot product means perpendicular. The same holds for b. The "crossed" indices in the formula are exactly what makes those terms cancel.

2. 长度：和点积是一对

2. Length: a partner of the dot product

展开可以验证一个恒等式：|a × b|² + (a · b)² = |a|² |b|²。点积是 |a||b|cos θ，代进去得 |a × b|² = |a|²|b|²(1 − cos²θ) = |a|²|b|² sin²θ。所以 |a × b| = |a||b| sin θ。点积量"沿着"的部分，叉积量"垂直"的部分，两者的平方加起来正好是 |a|²|b|²。

Expanding verifies an identity: |a × b|² + (a · b)² = |a|² |b|². The dot product is |a||b|cos θ, so |a × b|² = |a|²|b|²(1 − cos²θ) = |a|²|b|² sin²θ, and |a × b| = |a||b| sin θ. The dot product measures the "along" part and the cross product the "across" part; their squares add up to exactly |a|²|b|².

3. 为什么 |a||b| sin θ 是面积

3. Why |a||b| sin θ is an area

平行四边形的面积 = 底 × 高。底是 |a|，高是 b 垂直于 a 的那部分，也就是 |b| sin θ。把 b 沿着 a 的方向推来推去，高不变，面积就不变（游戏第 2 关会让你亲手验证）。

A parallelogram's area is base × height. The base is |a|; the height is the part of b perpendicular to a, which is |b| sin θ. Slide b along a's direction and the height, hence the area, stays the same (level 2 of the game has you check this yourself).

4. 物理：力矩

4. Physics: torque

用扳手拧螺丝，力矩 τ = r × F：r 是从螺丝指向手的向量，F 是你用的力。只有垂直于扳手的那部分力（|F| sin θ）在拧，力臂越长越省力；力矩的方向沿着螺丝的轴，用右手定则判断是拧紧还是拧松。

Turning a bolt with a wrench, the torque is τ = r × F, with r from the bolt to your hand and F your force. Only the part of the force perpendicular to the wrench (|F| sin θ) turns it, and a longer handle helps; the torque points along the bolt's axis, and the right-hand rule tells you whether it tightens or loosens.

function isFrontFacing(A, B, C, eye) {
  const n = B.sub(A).cross(C.sub(A));             // 只看正负，不用归一化
  return n.dot(eye.sub(A)) > 0;
}

      Pitfalls常见误区Common mistakes

      • 顺序写反。a × b 和 b × a 方向相反。三角形法线写成 (C − A) × (B − A)，所有面都会朝里，光照全黑，或者被背面剔除后整个模型看不见。
      • 以为叉积是数。3D 的叉积是向量。2D 里常说的"叉积"其实是它的 z 分量，一个带正负的数。
      • 忘了归一化。(B − A) × (C − A) 的长度是面积的 2 倍，不是 1。直接拿去做光照，大三角形会比小三角形亮。
      • 退化三角形。三点共线时叉积是零向量，归一化会得到 NaN。真实模型里经常有这种"细长到没有面积"的三角形。
      • 左手系和右手系。three.js、OpenGL 用右手坐标系；Unity 用左手坐标系。公式一样，但同样的数字在两种坐标系里画出来方向相反，在引擎之间搬数据时要小心。
      • Swapping the order. a × b and b × a point in opposite directions. Write a triangle normal as (C − A) × (B − A) and every face points inward: lighting goes black, or back-face culling makes the whole model vanish.
      • Treating it as a number. The 3D cross product is a vector. The "2D cross product" people mention is really its z component, a signed number.
      • Forgetting to normalize. (B − A) × (C − A) has length twice the area, not 1. Use it directly for lighting and large triangles come out brighter than small ones.
      • Degenerate triangles. With collinear points the cross product is the zero vector and normalizing gives NaN. Real meshes often contain such sliver triangles with no area.
      • Left- vs right-handed. three.js and OpenGL use right-handed coordinates; Unity uses left-handed. The formula is the same, but the same numbers point in opposite drawn directions, so take care when moving data between engines.
